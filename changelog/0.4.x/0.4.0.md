# 0.4.0 — 2026-10-08

## Added

- **`epa_get_air_quality`** reads `observation/current/ziplatlong/` and `forecast/current/`, returns per-site readings (`siteName`, `siteID`, `reportingAgency`), the agency `discussion`, and an `attribution` credits block ([#30](https://github.com/cyanheads/epa-mcp-server/issues/30)).
- **`epa_search_tri_releases`** takes opt-in `include_release_breakdown` for air, water, land, and underground-injection releases at the cost of one more upstream request ([#17](https://github.com/cyanheads/epa-mcp-server/issues/17)).
- **`epa_search_tri_releases`** records carry `countyName`; TRI quantities reported in grams (dioxin and dioxin-like compounds) are converted to pounds and carry `reportedUnit`.
- **Hosted endpoint** `https://epa.caseyjhand.com/mcp` is listed in `server.json` remotes and the README.

## Changed

- **`epa_get_air_quality` (breaking)** removes the `distance_miles` input and the `latitude`/`longitude` output fields, retypes `hourObserved`, and makes `aqi` optional (category-only forecasts omit it). `forecast_date` is optional, filters the issued forecast, and is rejected in current mode.
- **`epa_search_tri_releases`** is one DMAP join instead of an unbounded facility walk ([#32](https://github.com/cyanheads/epa-mcp-server/issues/32)); the `county` filter matches `county_name` after dropping a trailing County, Parish, or Borough ([#27](https://github.com/cyanheads/epa-mcp-server/issues/27)).
- **`epa_search_water_systems`** `has_violation` keeps systems with an open violation, in chunked lookups with a scan notice, and works with `zip_code`; `zip_code` is documented as the address of record.
- **`epa_get_facility`**, **`epa_search_facilities`**, **`epa_search_superfund`** render latitude and longitude independently ([#24](https://github.com/cyanheads/epa-mcp-server/issues/24)) and pound quantities at full precision.
- **`facility_id`** guidance on `epa_get_tri_releases` points at `epa_search_tri_releases` ([#21](https://github.com/cyanheads/epa-mcp-server/issues/21)).
- **Tests** assert exact tool sets, error messages, and per-field output across the suite.

## Fixed

- **DMAP row ranges** are 1-based and inclusive.
- **`epa_search_tri_releases`** under-limit searches no longer fail during enrichment.
